    Validation adds the extracted rows back up and compares them against the totals printed on each bill. For Northport that is the cover page's reference list and the charge lines from the detail pages — a shortfall on the second usually means detail pages are missing from the PDF. For Westports it is the charge lines against the pre-SST amount, the SST arithmetic, and the storage annex where one is attached.
